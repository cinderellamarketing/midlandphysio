1 What Capsule is for in your business ▸

Capsule sits alongside your practice management system, not instead of it. Patients, clinical notes and patient reporting stay where they are now. Capsule holds the business side of the practice:

  • Everyone you meet through networking, not only the people you end up working with
  • Your referral partners, and how those relationships are developing
  • The growth activity you do each week, such as one-to-ones, networking and content, so you can look back and see what has moved the business forward

Keeping the two apart was your call, and it means neither system gets cluttered with the other's contacts or skews the other's reporting.

In effect, Capsule takes over the job of your A2 desk pad: a record of the growth activity you do each day that you can look back on by the week, the month or longer.

1 Discovery call — 8 September 2026 ▸

What we covered

  • Why you started looking: your practice management system covers patients but not the business behind the practice.
  • Your A2 desk pad and the spreadsheet dashboard it led to.
  • How patients reach you, and why that side stays out of Capsule.
  • One-to-one notes, and the contacts you call feeders.
  • What you want to see: daily activity building into longer views, and progress against your targets.

Notes

Where this started. Patient data, clinical notes, scans and letters all live in your practice management system, which works well for patients but gives you no way to zoom in and out of the business itself. You designed a branded A2 desk pad with KPI metrics and daily tracking, found after a few days that it needed to be digital, and had a spreadsheet dashboard drafted. The question was whether a proper system would do that job better. You used Capsule years ago, and it has changed a great deal since.

How patients arrive. Patients book online, are referred to you, or ask you directly, for example at a BNI meeting, and your secretary books in anyone who rings the practice. There is no drawn-out sales process on that side, so patients stay in your practice management system. Clinicians who refer to you, such as surgeons, an anaesthetist and a podiatrist, are already kept there too, but general networking contacts, like a web designer you meet at BNI, belong in a CRM.

Activities and feeders. For each piece of growth activity you want to record who it was with, what you intended, what came of it and any follow-up, much as you would review patient notes before the next appointment. One-to-one notes currently go in the BNI notebook and are usually a line or two. The ones you do keep are for feeders: people who are a source of referrals rather than patients, such as commercial staff at two football clubs in your chapter.

Your growth actions fall into two groups: building relationships with partners who bring patients to you, and generating awareness to attract and convert patients directly. Networking is the only marketing you do at present, which suits a face-to-face, local business.

What you want to see is daily activity that builds into weekly, monthly, quarterly and longer views, with targets set against actuals, for example patients seen against a weekly target, so you can see the gap and decide what to do about it. That means looking back at what you have done and forward at the plan.

2 Capsule demo — 15 September 2026 ▸

What we covered

  • A walkthrough of a demo Capsule account set up around your referral partnerships and networking, with sample data in it.
  • Keeping patients in your practice management system and using Capsule for the business side only.
  • How referral partners and potential patients could sit in a sales pipeline, and whether they need a value.
  • Logging growth activities, and using goals and reports to see what each week has achieved.
  • The mobile app, the Outlook sidebar, Capsule plans, and how the implementation works.

Notes

Contacts and referral partners. In the demo, every contact had fields for their networking group, the date of your last one-to-one and the number of referrals received. A referral partner tag added a second set of fields, partner type, relationship stage and next action, which only appear on contacts that carry the tag. That keeps ordinary contacts uncluttered.

Two kinds of opportunity. You want both in view: referral partnerships, and individuals from networking who may become patients. A referral partnership has no fixed value, so it is measured by how many you convert. A potential patient can carry an average value, knowing that two patients with the same condition may need a different number of sessions. Two ways of handling this were shown: one pipeline with each opportunity tagged as a referral partner or potential patient so you can filter between them, or two separate pipelines, which needs the higher Capsule plan.

Activities, goals and reports. The activity types are deliberately descriptive so you can report on what you did by type over any period. Goals look forward, for example an activity goal of two one-to-ones a month, and reports look back. Activity that is not about another person, such as publishing a blog, can be logged against your own contact record.

A one-to-one is logged as an activity. You can type notes live, or photograph handwritten notes and attach the photo, then add any follow-up as a task. Pinning a note keeps it at the top of the record, so the next one-to-one with that person picks up where the last one left off.

Tasks show as a list and in the calendar. The schedule view shows your week, and you can drag tasks to a different day when networking or clinics fill it up.

With Office 365, the Outlook sidebar lets you file emails against a contact, send email from Capsule and add tasks straight from your inbox. Capsule also has a mobile app for phone and iPad.

Once it is built, you will be able to add and change activity types, custom fields and tags yourself, for example a new growth activity that was not on your original list.

3 Setup session — 24 September 2026 ▸

What we covered

  • Opened your Capsule account on the Starter plan and showed where to add your card.
  • Your one-to-one process and the follow-ups that come out of it.
  • The networking groups you are part of.
  • Your referral partners: who they are, how a partnership forms, and what you want to track.
  • Where contacts who are a way in, rather than a partner, should sit.

Notes

Your account. Starter plan, paid monthly, with a 14-day free trial. Add a card under Account Settings, then Account and Plan. Nothing is charged until the trial ends. Your previous Capsule account is closed, which is why the new web address has a slightly different ending, so bookmark it.

One-to-ones. You arrange them by message, email, phone or in person, and you would rather type notes live on your laptop and save them than keep a notebook. Follow-ups, such as adding someone to a WhatsApp group or making an introduction, can be set as tasks in Capsule, since you prefer electronic reminders to a notebook. Introductions do not need a separate system: a short note on the contact, such as "Intro to" and the name, is date and time stamped, so six months later you can ask how it went.

Networking groups. BNI, and Pro Networking, which you have recently joined and which brings you people outside BNI. Groups you are only visiting go down as Other until you join them.

Referral partners come mainly through networking, plus partners you already work with across the clinics, some for many years, and direct approaches, such as a spinal surgeon who replied to an email and asked to meet. The types include podiatrists, anaesthetists, sports medicine doctors, GPs, personal trainers, sports massage therapists and sports clubs, and the list will grow as new ones come on board.

A partnership forms through an approach, a call or meeting, sometimes an in-person follow-up, and then an informal arrangement to refer to each other when it is clinically appropriate. There is no formal agreement and no numbers attached, and as regulated professionals there is no financial incentive. Contact carries on through progress updates and discharge summaries sent back to the referrer.

What you want to see on each partner is how many patients have come from them and how long the relationship has run. The start of the relationship will be recorded as the month and year it was agreed, so you can see its length at a glance without opening the record. Some partners will refer rarely or not at all, and a relationship can be silent for months before a referral arrives, which is normal.

Contacts who are a way in rather than a partner, such as people at a football club where you sponsor a player, stay as contacts with tasks against them and do not go into the pipeline.

You use a Mac, with Mac Mail for email and Dropbox for files. Your contacts are currently on business cards and place mats, and you will add them to Capsule yourself.
